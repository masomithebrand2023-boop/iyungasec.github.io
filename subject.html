Subjects Offered

Discover the academic and technical subjects offered at Iyunga Secondary School, designed to build knowledge, practical skills and prepare students for future opportunities.

OUR CURRICULUM

Learning That Builds the Future

Iyunga Secondary School provides students with opportunities to develop both academic knowledge and practical technical skills. Our subjects encourage critical thinking, creativity, problem-solving and preparation for further education and professional development.

Academic Subjects

Subjects available for students in Form I – IV.

Mathematics

Developing logical thinking and problem-solving skills.

Science

Understanding the natural world through scientific knowledge.

English

Building communication, reading and writing skills.

Kiswahili

Strengthening language, communication and cultural understanding.

Geography

Exploring people, places, environment and the Earth.

History

Learning about past events and their influence on society.

Physics

Understanding matter, energy, motion and natural phenomena.

Chemistry

Studying substances, reactions and chemical processes.

Biology

Exploring living organisms, health and biological systems.

Business Studies

Introducing students to business, entrepreneurship and commerce.

Architecture Drawing

Developing technical drawing and design skills.

Technical Subjects

Practical and technology-oriented areas of study.

Computer Science

Learning programming, computing concepts and digital technologies.

Electronics

Understanding electronic components, circuits and systems.

Mechanics

Developing knowledge of machines, mechanical systems and tools.

Cyber Security

Introduction to digital security, threats and safe computing.

Networking

Learning how computers and devices communicate across networks.

Computer Maintenance

Developing skills in computer maintenance and troubleshooting.
