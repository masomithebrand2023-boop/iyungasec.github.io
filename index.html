IYUNGA SECONDARY SCHOOL

Building Knowledge, Character and a Brighter Future

Welcome to Iyunga Secondary School, an institution committed to providing quality secondary education while nurturing academic excellence, discipline, creativity, leadership and responsible citizenship among students.

Iyunga Secondary School provides an environment where students are encouraged to discover their abilities, develop their talents and prepare themselves for higher education and future careers. Through effective teaching, continuous learning, discipline and participation in various school activities, students are given opportunities to grow academically, socially and personally.

OUR LOCATION

Iyunga, Mbeya, Tanzania

Iyunga Secondary School is located in the Iyunga area of Mbeya, in the southern highland zone of Tanzania. Its location makes it part of the educational community of Mbeya, a region well known for its contribution to education, agriculture, commerce and other economic activities. The school's location provides students with an environment where education can be connected with the social, economic and environmental realities of the surrounding community.
